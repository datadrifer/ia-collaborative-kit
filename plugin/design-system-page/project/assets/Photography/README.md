# Photography

IA's own photography, from iacollaborative.com: its Chicago studio, its model shop and its team at work. Real places and real work, in natural light. Lead every deck, page and one-pager with one of them; IA is photo-led, and an all-type piece reads as a template.

- Choose the photograph for the subject, and do not repeat one across the pieces of one project.
- Untinted and unfiltered. Never a blue overlay, a duotone or a gradient wash.
- Always in a 14px-cornered frame (`radius-containers`); full bleed only on a deck cover, a page hero or a divider.
- White type over a photo sits on the black scrim, never straight on the picture.
- The four studio photographs are 2400px wide, from IA's originals: sharp on a full-bleed slide. The three work photographs are web copies; ask IA for originals before full-bleed print.
- Client work and case-study images are not here and never stand in for IA's own photography.
