# Brand elements

IA's own marks and grounds, beyond the logo. All are IA's files from its site.

| File | Ink | What it is | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ia-asterisk.svg` | IA blue #176cd9 | IA's signature six-spoke asterisk. | Marks the one statement that matters: at the cap height of an `h2`, or a 60px corner mark on a grey panel. One per page, slide or panel. White or `card` grounds only; never on the blue panel, a photo or black; never a bullet or pattern. In code it is a CSS mask filled with `primary`. |
| `ia-insight-to-action.svg` | White #ffffff | The brand line as drawn lettering. | Full width across the home hero over photography or film, a hairline and a `small` caption under it. Once per piece, at the top. Never retyped. |
| `ia-insight-to-action-black.svg` | Black #000000 | The same lettering for white grounds. | The one-pager masthead, a print cover, a white opening slide. Once, full measure. |
| `ia-blue-field-1.png` | IA blue into white | The soft blue field behind the conversational panel. | The ground of one quiet panel, the blue mass low and off an edge. Text on the white side only. Never tiled, tinted or cropped to the blue. |
| `ia-blue-field-2.jpg` | IA blue into white | A wider blur of IA blue rising from the lower left. | A deck's section divider or one quiet panel, full bleed, once per page or deck section. Black text on the white side only. |

These two fields are IA's only gradients. Never paint one in CSS.
